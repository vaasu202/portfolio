# AgentGuard model image analysis

## Identification

The source is a product-interface screenshot, not a photograph of a physical object. It is suitable as brand and interaction evidence for a stylized procedural browser prop, but not as geometric reconstruction evidence. The proposed object is a policy gateway derived from the visible AgentGuard identity.

## Observable form language

- The interface uses repeated square and rectangular frames, one central diamond boundary, circular runtime nodes, thin connection rails, and a shield mark.
- The strongest silhouette cue is the central diamond nested inside a circular inspection boundary.
- The composition is approximately bilateral around the policy boundary, with ingress on one side and protected infrastructure on the other.

## Macro, meso, and micro hierarchy

- Macro: beveled policy core, octagonal inspection ring, ingress rail, quarantine branch, protected egress node.
- Meso: four ring clamps, central shield plate, packet carrier, redirection hinge, egress socket.
- Micro: lime edge inlay, shallow policy grooves, three status pins, packet seam, quarantine stop marker.

## Spatial relationships

- The policy core sits inside the inspection ring.
- The ingress rail is socketed into the ring at the negative-X side.
- The protected egress node is socketed into the positive-X side.
- The quarantine branch pivots down and forward from the inspection ring.
- The packet travels on the ingress rail, stops at the core, then redirects into quarantine.

## Materials and finish

- Main structure: near-black satin metal, medium roughness, subtle clearcoat.
- Edge structure: dark graphite metal with higher roughness.
- Policy inlay and accepted-state details: lime dielectric coating with restrained emissive response.
- Blocked-state packet and quarantine marker: warm red-orange dielectric coating derived from the screenshot.
- Pins and rails: bone-white ceramic or coated polymer.

## Identity features

- Shield or policy-boundary silhouette.
- Central inspection boundary with a visible stop decision.
- Ingress packet that cannot pass through unchecked.
- Redirected quarantine path that makes the block action understandable.

## Uncertainty and limits

- The screenshot does not describe hidden 3D surfaces, depth, bevel size, or back construction.
- Geometry, back-face construction, and exact materials are intentional stylized approximations.
- The target is a compact real-time explanatory prop, not an exact reconstruction of a physical object.
