# Night Gallery assets

Models downloaded from the Three.js r185 examples (https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/r185/examples).

- Lucy100k.ply: Lucy scan distributed in the Three.js spotlight example; original scan from the Stanford 3D Scanning Repository (https://graphics.stanford.edu/data/3Dscanrep/).
- stormtrooper.dae and Stormtrooper_D.jpg: Dancing Stormtrooper by StrykerDoesAnimation (https://sketchfab.com/strykerdoesgames), CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), as credited in the Three.js Collada skinning example. Adaptations: lighting, normalized scale, staging and user-controlled playback.
- Other models are the existing procedural portfolio assets, reused without replacement.

The gallery's masked word reveals, stagger timing, hero exit, and smooth chapter progression are adapted from the user-supplied Kage HTML reference. No Kage background, environment assets or ThreeUI package is included.


BB-8: procedural geometry and weathered materials supplied by the user in `1bd1ec2e-98ab-4e9a-ac1b-0bdcec2a8f3e/pasted-text.txt`. Only the body and head model are integrated; its separate page, floor, camera, hearts and audio are omitted. Static details are merged by material without changing the supplied geometry. BB-8 is a Star Wars character owned by Lucasfilm.
